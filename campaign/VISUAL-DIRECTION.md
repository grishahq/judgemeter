# Reusable campaign visual direction

Use navy (#101827), teal (#64dcc0), amber (#e4b660) and cream. Prefer editorial paper/mechanical metaphors for the conceptual posts: one fixed document, repeated judgment paths, precise instruments that can still miss a target. Use large English headlines with restrained typography and enough negative space for mobile reading. Keep JudgeMeter naming consistent.

Concept images must remain labeled as illustrations in their alt text. Never generate dashboards, experimental charts, customer logos, adoption figures, testimonials or benchmark screenshots. Preserve imagegen prompts and source paths in ART-PROVENANCE.json. Actual demo imagery comes from the software; empirical-looking numbers must be derived from raw exported data with plotting tools and carry the synthetic-data disclosure.

For evidence posts, keep the scope statement in the image itself: synthetic demo, 12 fixed cases, six trials, seed 42, threshold 0.5; no measured-model or human-label accuracy comparison. Use the real report screenshot for workflow/quickstart beats and the data-derived chart for the methodology beat. Retain readable labels at social feed scale.
